Local Community Gardening Initiative - Site Plan

Site Name

Local Community Gardening Initiative

This name reflects the focus on community involvement in gardening, promoting sustainability, and fostering local connections.

Optional domain availability: localcommunitygardening.org

Site Purpose

The site serves as a hub for local gardening enthusiasts, providing resources, guides, and information about community gardening events and opportunities for engagement.

Scenarios

  • What resources are available for starting my own community garden?
  • How can I participate in upcoming gardening workshops and events?

Color Schema

  • Green (#4CAF50): Used for headings and accents to represent nature and growth.
  • Dark Gray (#555555): Used for body text to ensure readability and contrast against the background.

Typography

  • Headings: Arial, sans-serif (used for all headings)
  • Body Text: Arial, sans-serif (used for all paragraph text)
